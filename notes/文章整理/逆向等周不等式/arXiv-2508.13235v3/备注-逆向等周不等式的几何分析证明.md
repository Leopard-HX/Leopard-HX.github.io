## 二、摘要（Abstract，原文照录）

> We present a proof of the reverse isoperimetric inequality—a central conjecture in extended black hole thermodynamics—for black holes in Einstein gravity with $D \ge 4$, employing a two-pronged geometric–analytic method. Our analysis shows that the reversal of the usual isoperimetric inequality originates from the structure of curved backgrounds governed by Einstein's equations, thereby underscoring the fundamental role of gravity in the reverse isoperimetric property of AdS black hole horizons.

## 四、已核对要点

1. **两条并行的证明路线**（Sec. I）：一条用**引力聚焦**（gravitational focusing）配合 **Sherif–Dunsby 共形刚性定理**；另一条独立地通过**切片泛函（slice functional）的第二变分**分析。
   > 原文（Sec. I）："Using gravitational focusing together with the Sherif–Dunsby conformal rigidity theorem, and independently through a second–variation analysis of the slice functional, we show that the round horizon is the unique entropy maximizer among all hypersurfaces enclosing a fixed thermodynamic volume."

2. **核心结论（静态情形）**：在"包围固定热力学体积"的所有超曲面中，**圆球形视界是唯一的熵最大化子**（Sec. I、Sec. II 标题）。

3. **核心结论（旋转情形）**：把 Kerr–AdS 视为圆球在固定热力学体积下的**离壳形变**，证明该形变必然使熵**低于** Schwarzschild–AdS 的情形（Sec. I、Sec. III.1）。
   > 原文（Sec. I）："We first treat Kerr–AdS as an off–shell deformation of the round sphere at fixed thermodynamic volume and show that such a deformation necessarily lowers the entropy relative to the Schwarzschild–AdS case."

4. **几何量约定**：所用"几何体积"在静态情形下**与热力学体积一致**（Sec. I）。
   > 原文（Sec. I）："…geometric volume, which coincides with the thermodynamic volume in the static case."

5. **刚性定理出处**：A. M. Sherif, P. K. S. Dunsby, *Conformal geometry on a class of embedded hypersurfaces in spacetimes*, Class. Quant. Grav. **39** (2022) 045004, arXiv:2112.08753 [math.DG]（= 本文文献 **[19]**，见参考文献表）；配合 Appendix A 的**定理 A.1** 使用。

6. **Appendix D 的关键等式**：推广 Dolan 的识别 $I_E = \beta G$（欧氏作用量 = $\beta \times$ 吉布斯自由能）到旋转的巨正则边界数据，并**显式用欧氏方法导出 Kerr–AdS 的热力学体积**；文中指出该体积与黑洞化学中使用的（Komar / Iyer–Wald）体积相同。

> 说明：以上仅为**已逐句核对过**的部分；定理 A.1 的完整陈述与证明细节待补，见第七节。
