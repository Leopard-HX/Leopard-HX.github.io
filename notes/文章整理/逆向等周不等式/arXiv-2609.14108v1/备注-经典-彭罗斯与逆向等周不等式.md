## 二、摘要（Abstract，原文照录）

> A collection of evidence is presented showing that the conjectured reverse isoperimetric inequality (RII) for asymptotically anti-de Sitter (AdS) as well as de Sitter (dS) black holes, $\mathcal{R}_{\text{RII}} \ge 1$, is of fundamental importance and its violation ($\mathcal{R}_{\text{RII}} < 1$) gives rise to either thermodynamic instabilities, or physically unreasonable solutions, or naked singularities. We show that AdS black holes violating reverse isoperimetric inequality, known as superentropic black holes, satisfy neither mechanical stability requirement of $\kappa_T \ge \kappa_S \ge 0$ nor thermal stability requirement of $C_P \ge C_V \ge 0$. This property makes them thermodynamically unstable for the whole range of parameter space and explains all the diverse behaviors so far reported. We conjecture this statement holds for all superentropic black holes. We confirm that there is no counterexample to the thermodynamic instability conjecture of superentropic black holes and extend this to dS space by presenting the first example of superentropicity in dS space. By bringing evidence, we then propose two other conjectures: 1) there is no asymptotically flat limit of superentropic black holes, and 2) there exist two stronger versions of Penrose isoperimetric inequality (PII) for asymptotically flat black holes; the first is the asymptotically flat limit of the RII and the second is a new inequality in terms of the ADM mass and the thermodynamics volume, that we call thermo-volumetric inequality, obtained via insights from the $\Lambda \to 0$ limit of extended black hole thermodynamics in AdS. We show that the Penrose isoperimetric inequality (PII) is weaker than the reverse isoperimetric inequality (RII), as demonstrated explicitly for $D = 4, 5$ and for all black hole families we have examined.

## 四、已核对要点

1. **记号**：逆向等周不等式写作

   $$
   \mathcal{R}_{\text{RII}} \ge 1
   $$

   其违反（$\mathcal{R}_{\text{RII}} < 1$）对应"超熵（superentropic）黑洞"（Abstract、Sec. I 标题）。

2. **违反 RII 的三种后果**：热力学不稳定、物理上不合理的解、或裸奇点（Abstract）。

3. **主定理式结论**：违反 RII 的 AdS 黑洞（即超熵黑洞）**既不满足力学稳定性要求 $\kappa_T \ge \kappa_S \ge 0$，也不满足热稳定性要求 $C_P \ge C_V \ge 0$**；这使其在**整个参数区间**上热力学不稳定，并解释了文献中已报道的各种纷杂行为（Abstract、Sec. III）。作者**猜想这适用于所有超熵黑洞**，并称未发现反例。

4. **首次给出 dS 空间的超熵例子**（Abstract、Sec. III）——即本文把超熵性从 AdS 推广到 dS。

5. **另外两个猜想**（Abstract、Sec. IV 与 Sec. V）：
   1. **超熵黑洞不存在渐近平坦极限**（Sec. IV）；
   2. 渐近平坦黑洞存在**两个更强的 Penrose 等周不等式（PII）版本**：第一个是 **RII 的渐近平坦极限**，第二个是用 **ADM 质量与热力学体积**表述的新不等式，作者称之为 **thermo-volumetric inequality**，来自 AdS 扩展黑洞热力学取 $\Lambda \to 0$ 极限的启示（Sec. V–VI）。

6. **PII 弱于 RII**：对 $D = 4, 5$ 及所考察的全部黑洞族**显式验证**（Abstract）。

7. **背景链条**（Sec. I）：渐近平坦带电荷/旋转黑洞的半经典第一定律与 Euler 关系（Smarr 公式）分别为

   $$
   \mathrm{d}M = T\,\mathrm{d}S + \Omega\,\mathrm{d}J + \Phi\,\mathrm{d}Q, \qquad M = 2TS + 2\Omega J + \Phi Q
   $$

   （几何单位 $G_N = c = k_B = \hbar = 1$；$S,\Omega,J,\Phi,Q$ 分别为熵、角速度、角动量、电势与电荷）。随后引出四维的 **Penrose 等周不等式（PII）** 作为"平直时空下形状–热力学"的著名联系。
   > 原文（Sec. I）："A remarkable connection between the shape and thermodynamics of asymptotically flat black holes is the Penrose Isoperimetric Inequality (PII) in four-dimensions…"

8. **不再公开细节的部分**：Sec. VI 标题含 "$P = 0$"，对应把扩展热力学压强取零（即 $\Lambda \to 0$）后对渐近平坦 Lovelock 黑洞的处理。

> 说明：以上仅为**已逐句核对过**的部分；Secs. III–VI 的具体计算（各黑洞族的 $\kappa_T,\kappa_S,C_P,C_V$ 表达式、dS 例子）待补，见第六节。

## 五、与同组论文的关系

- 本文与 `arXiv-2508.13235v3`、`arXiv-2606.28427v1`（Naman Kumar 的两篇）**没有引用关系**：本文全文（PDF 文本抽取）中 `Kumar`、`Sherif`、`Dunsby` 出现次数均为 **0**。

- **角度差异**：Kumar 两篇走**数学结构路线**（几何–分析证明 / Noether 荷稳定性定理，直接"证明 RII"）；本文走**现象学/反例检验路线**（把 RII 当成判据，用 $\kappa_T \ge \kappa_S \ge 0$ 与 $C_P \ge C_V \ge 0$ 把"违反 RII ⇒ 不稳定"串起来，并推广到 dS 与渐近平坦）。
