### 1.1 [03749] 的几何与准局部物态方程

1. 考虑四维渐近平坦 Schwarzschild 黑洞置于有限球形腔中，度规由 $f(r)=1-r_h/r$ 给出，腔位于 $r_B>r_h$。
   [引·03749 §II："We consider a four-dimensional, asymptotically flat Schwarzschild black hole enclosed in a finite spherical cavity. The spacetime metric is given by … $f(r) = 1 - \frac{r_h}{r}\,$"；"The spherical cavity is located at the radius $r_B>r_h$."]

2. 熵由 Bekenstein–Hawking 公式给出，热力学体积取球形腔的面积：$S=\pi r_h^2/G$，$V=4\pi r_B^2$。
   [引·03749 式(2)："S = \frac{\pi r_h^{2}}{G}\,, \qquad V = 4\pi r_B^{2}\,."]

3. 内能 $E$ 等于准局部 Brown–York 能量，写成 $(S,V)$ 的函数为 $E(S,V)=\frac{1}{2G}\sqrt{\frac{V}{\pi}}\left[1-\sqrt{1-\sqrt{\tfrac{4GS}{V}}}\right]$。
   [引·03749 式(3)："The internal energy $E$ is equal to the quasi-local Brown-York energy [York1990,Brown1993], which can be written in terms of the state variables $(S,V)$ as" + 式(3)]

4. 边界温度是 $r=r_B$ 处观者看到的红移 Hawking 温度，即 Tolman 温度，$T(S,V)=\frac{1}{4\sqrt{\pi G S}\,\sqrt{1-\sqrt{4GS/V}}}$。
   [引·03749 式(4)："Similarly, the local temperature at the boundary is the redshifted Hawking temperature seen by an observer at $r=r_B$, also known as the Tolman temperature, which as a function of $(S,V)$ is" + 式(4)]

5. 全息压强 $P$ 是腔壁上的 Brown–York 表面压力，$P(S,V)=\frac{1}{4G\sqrt{\pi V}}\left[\frac{1-\tfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{4GS/V}}{\sqrt{1-\sqrt{4GS/V}}}-1\right]$。
   [引·03749 式(5)："According to [Banihashemi:2024yye,Borsboom:2026ash], the holographic pressure $P$ is the Brown-York surface pressure [York1990,Brown1993] on the cavity wall" + 式(5)]

6. 基本关系为 $dE=T\,dS-P\,dV$。
   [引·03749 式(1)："d E = T d S - P d V\,."]

7. 对 $E$ 与 $P$ 都做了平直空间背景减除，使它们在 Minkowski 时空（$r_h=0$）中恒为零。
   [引·03749 §II："A flat-space background subtraction is applied to both $E$ and $P$, so that they vanish identically in Minkowski spacetime, for which $r_h = 0$."]

8. 除能量表示外，[03749] 还使用 Helmholtz（正则）表示：独立变量为 $(T,V)$，势为 $F=E-TS$，$dF=-S\,dT-P\,dV$；以及焓表示：独立变量为 $(S,P)$，势为 $H=E+PV$，$dH=T\,dS+V\,dP$。
   [引·03749 §II："In the \emph{Helmholtz (canonical) representation}, the independent variables are the temperature $T$ and the volume $V$, and the thermodynamic potential is the Helmholtz free energy $F = E - TS$"，"and its variation yields $d F = -S d T - P d V$"；"In the \emph{enthalpy representation}, the two independent variables are the entropy $S$ and the pressure $P$, and the thermodynamic potential is the enthalpy $H = E + PV$"，"$d H = T d S + V d P\,$"]

### 1.2 [03749] 的分支结构与临界温度

9. 反解 Tolman 温度方程得到关于视界半径的三次方程 $r_h^3-r_Br_h^2+\frac{r_B}{(4\pi T)^2}=0$。
   [引·03749 式(6)："In four spacetime dimensions, the Tolman temperature equation \eqref{eq:T} can be rearranged into a cubic equation for the horizon radius" + 式(6)]

10. York 证明：当 $r_BT>\sqrt{27}/(8\pi)$ 时，该三次方程在物理区间 $0<r_h<r_B$ 内有两个不同的正实根；引入 $x\equiv r_h/r_B=\sqrt{4GS/V}$ 后，两根对应小黑洞分支 $0<x<2/3$ 与大黑洞分支 $2/3<x<1$。
    [引·03749 §II："York [York1985a,York1990] showed that this cubic equation admits two distinct positive, real roots in the physical range $0<r_h<r_B$ when $r_B T>\sqrt{27}/(8\pi)$. Introducing $x\equiv r_h/r_B=\sqrt{4GS/V}$, these roots correspond to the small black hole branch $0<x<2/3$ and the large black hole branch $2/3<x<1$."]

11. 大分支（$2/3<x<1$）的定容热容 $C_V>0$，在固定边界体积下准静态热交换是局部稳定的；小黑洞分支 $C_V<0$。
    [引·03749 §II："The fixed volume heat capacity is positive for $2/3<x<1$, so this branch is locally stable under quasi-static heat exchange at fixed boundary volume. By contrast, the small black hole branch has $C_V<0$: a temperature fluctuation induced by heat exchange is amplified rather than damped, making this branch unsuitable as a passively stable working substance."]

12. 定义无量纲红移参数 $y\equiv\sqrt{1-r_h/r_B}$（$0<y<1$），它度量视界与腔壁之间的引力红移。
    [引·03749 式(11)："y \equiv \sqrt{1 - \frac{r_h}{r_B}}\,, \qquad 0 < y < 1"；"which measures the gravitational redshift between the horizon and the cavity wall."]

13. [03749] 给出（在其被注释掉的段落中）在壳自由能 $F=\frac{r_B}{4Gy}(1-y)(3y-1)$，并指出热平直空间（hot flat space）的自由能为零、大 Schwarzschild 鞍点在 $F<0$（等价于 $x>8/9$）时主导。
    [引·03749 注释块："Using equations~\eqref{eq:U}, \eqref{eq:T}, and $F=E-TS$, together with the redshift variable $y=\sqrt{1-x}$, the free energy of the Schwarzschild saddle is $F = \frac{r_B}{4Gy}(1-y)(3y-1).$"；"With the same background subtraction, hot flat space has vanishing classical free energy. The large Schwarzschild saddle therefore has lower Euclidean action and dominates over hot flat space when $F<0$, equivalently $x>\frac{8}{9}.$"]

14. 文中正文的热机效率与循环图全部限制在大黑洞分支。
    [引·03749 §IV："All efficiencies and cycle diagrams in the main text are restricted to the large black hole branch, $x=r_h/r_B>2/3$."]

### 1.3 [25791] 的广义自由能与速率公式

15. 广义自由能（热势）定义为 $\mathcal{U}=\int(T_h-T)\,dS$。
    [引·25791 式(17)："Now, based on the definition of generalized free energy in reference [29, 30, 31, 32], we release the Maxwell equal area law mentioned above, and introduce generalized free energy 𝒰 (or called the thermal potential) as" + 式(17)]

16. 该构造是在等压条件下、从 $T$–$S$ 图出发、放松平衡约束得到的离壳自由能。
    [引·25791 §III："For generalized free energy (17), we can understand it is constructed under isobaric conditions, starting from the T−S diagram, by relaxing the equilibrium constraint and allowing the relevant parameters to vary independently."]

17. 其极值条件为 $d\mathcal{U}/dS=0\Rightarrow T=T_h$；极小值对应热稳定黑洞态，极大值对应热不稳定态。
    [引·25791 式(18)："d𝒰/dS = d/dS (∫(Th−T)dS) = 0 ⇒ T = Th"；"The minima of the generalized free energy landscape correspond to thermally stable black hole states, whereas the maxima signify thermally unstable ones."]

18. 另有 $\mathcal{U}=\int(T_h-T)dS=E+PV-TS=M-TS=\mathcal{G}$，故固定压强时 $\mathcal{U}$ 与离壳 Gibbs 自由能 $\mathcal{G}=M-TS$ 数学上完全相同。
    [引·25791 式(19)+§III："𝒰=∫(Th−T)dS = E+PV−TS = M−TS = 𝒢"；"Thus, when P is fixed, 𝒰 and 𝒢 are mathematically identical."]

19. [25791] 列出三个离壳势：$\mathcal{U}$（$T$–$S$ 平面，以环境温度为离壳变量）、$\mathcal{G}$（$G$–$T$/$G$–$P$ 平面）、以及 Landau 势 $\mathcal{L}=\int F(X,T,P)\,dX$（$P$–$V$ 平面，以非平衡体积 $X$ 为离壳变量）；并明说在 $P$–$V$ 分析中 $\mathcal{L}$ 更自然，物理结论与选择无关。
    [引·25791 式(20)+§III："The three off-shell potentials differ in which variable is taken off-shell and which thermodynamic plane they naturally belong to. 𝒰 (on the T-S plane) takes the ambient temperature as the off-shell variable; 𝒢 (on the G-T or G-P plane) generalizes the Gibbs free energy by lifting the constraint T=Th; ℒ (on the P-V plane) uses the non‑equilibrium volume X as the off-shell variable."；"In this work we adopt 𝒰 because our analysis focuses on the T-S plane; for G-T/G-P or P-V analyses, 𝒢 or ℒ would be more natural. The physical conclusions are equivalent regardless of the choice."]

20. 随机动力学用过阻尼 Fokker–Planck 方程描述，序参量为事件视界半径 $r$，$\beta=1/T$ 为逆温度，$D$ 为扩散系数。
    [引·25791 式(21)+§III："∂ρ(r,t)/∂t = D ∂/∂r( e^{−β𝒰(r)} ∂/∂r( e^{β𝒰(r)}ρ(r,t) ) )"；"where the ρ(r,t) is the probability distribution of black hole states, β=1/T denotes the inverse temperature, 𝒰(r) is the generalized free energy in our work, and D is the diffusion coefficient. The radius of the black hole event horizon r is regarded as a order parameter of the system."]

21. 反射边界取在初始态 $r_A$（概率流为零），吸收边界取在刚越过热势极大的点 $r_m$（概率密度为零），此时相变视为完成。
    [引·25791 §III："Applying a reflecting boundary condition at rA means that the probability flux j at rA is zero, indicating a reflective boundary where no states flow out of the system. rA is typically chosen as the initial state for the phase transition dynamics."；"And rm is usually chosen as the point that just crosses the maximum of the thermal potential."]

22. MFPT 的精确积分式为 $\langle t\rangle=\frac{1}{D}\int_{r_A}^{r_m}dr\int_{r_A}^{r}dr'\,e^{\beta(\mathcal{U}(r)-\mathcal{U}(r'))}$。
    [引·25791 式(26)]

23. Kramers 逃逸率的近似解析式为 $r_k=\frac{\sqrt{|\mathcal{U}''(r_{\min})\mathcal{U}''(r_{\max})|}}{2\pi}e^{-\frac{\mathcal{U}(r_{\max})-\mathcal{U}(r_{\min})}{D}}$。
    [引·25791 式(32)："and we may extend the above two integrations boundaries to ±∞, thus the Kramers escape rate can be taken as [36, 37]" + 式(32)]

24. 二者物理含义不同（逃逸率是单位时间翻越势垒的态所占比例，MFPT 是单个态越过势垒所需的平均时间），因此近似互为倒数。
    [引·25791 §III："Physically, the Kramers escape rate represents the fraction of states that successfully surmount the potential barrier per unit time, whereas the MFPT denotes the average time a single state requires to cross the barrier and reach the target state. Consequently, they are approximately reciprocals of each other, as can be readily verified by comparing Eqs. (26) and (29)."]

25. Kramers 解析式只在深阱条件下成立：$\Delta\mathcal{U}=\mathcal{U}(r_{\max})-\mathcal{U}(r_{\min})\gg D$。
    [引·25791 §IV："In general, this requires the thermal potential to satisfy the deep well condition, namely Δ𝒰=𝒰(rmax)−𝒰(rmin)≫D."]

26. MFPT 用精确积分以保证数值精度，Kramers 用近似解析式，后者是高垒极限下的领头阶近似。
    [引·25791 §III："We adopt the exact integral formulation for the MFPT to ensure numerical accuracy across the whole landscape. In contrast, for the Kramers escape rate, we use the approximate analytical form, as it represents a leading-order approximation in the high-barrier limit."]

27. [25791] 在扩展相空间中的推广第一定律与 Smarr 关系为 $dM=T_hdS+VdP+\Phi_mdQ_m+\phi_mdm_0+\phi_qdq_0$、$M=2T_hS-2VP+\Phi_mQ_m+\phi_mm_0+\phi_qq_0$。
    [引·25791 式(13)、(15)]

28. 但 [25791] 中与压强共轭的体积取 $V=\tfrac43\pi r_+^3$，即挂在视界半径 $r_+$ 上。
    [引·25791 式(9)："V=43πr+3"（即 $V=\frac43\pi r_+^3$）]

29. [25791] 的中心问题是：相变过程中正则黑洞究竟（亚）稳定中间态还是不稳定的过渡态。
    [引·25791 §I："Therefore, our central question is: Is the regular black hole characterized as a stable (or metastable) intermediate or an unstable transition state during the phase transition process?"]

## 7. 离壳自由能图景（V9–V11）

44. 按 [25791] 的精神，在固定 $V$（即固定 $r_B$）与固定边界温度 $T$ 下引入离壳自由能

    $$
    F_{\rm off}(r_h;T,r_B)=E(S(r_h),V)-T\,S(r_h),
    $$

    其中 $T$ 视为环境温度、$r_h$ 为序参量。这正是 [03749] 的 Helmholtz 表示（其势为 $F=E-TS$）。
    [引·25791 式(17) 的构造精神；引·03749 §II 的 Helmholtz 表示 $F=E-TS$、$dF=-SdT-PdV$；推·固定 $V$ 时 $dE=T_hdS$，故 $\int(T_h-T)dS=E-TS+\text{const}=F$]

45. 定义无量纲量 $\tau\equiv\pi r_BT$ 与 $g(y)\equiv G\,F_{\rm off}/r_B$，则

    $$
    g(y)=1-y-\tau\,(1-y^2)^2.
    $$

    [算·V4 给出 $E=r_B(1-y)/G$；把 $S=\pi r_B^2(1-y^2)^2/G$ 代入即得，[算·V9–V11] 中所有 $g$ 的运算均基于此式]

46. 一阶导与二阶导为

    $$
    g'(y)=-1+4\tau y(1-y^2),\qquad g''(y)=4\tau(1-3y^2).

    [算·V9：`Simplify[D[g[y,tau],y]]` 输出 $-1-4\tau y(-1+y^2)$，即 $-1+4\tau y(1-y^2)$；V10：`D[g[y,tau],{y,2}]` 输出 $-\tau(8y^2-4(1-y^2))=4\tau(1-3y^2)$]

    [算·V9：`Solve[D[g[y,tau],y]==0,tau]` 输出 $\{\tau\to-(-1)/(4y(-1+y^2))\}$，即 $\tau=1/(4y(1-y^2))$]

    [算·V10 的 $g''$ 表达式；引·03749 §II 关于 $C_V$ 的陈述；引·25791："The minima … correspond to thermally stable black hole states, whereas the maxima signify thermally unstable ones."]

## 13. 与 [25791] 三个离壳势的对应

76. 本方案采用的 $F_{\rm off}(r_h;T,r_B)=E-TS$ 就是 [03749] 的 Helmholtz 势 $F=E-TS$，而它与 [25791] 的 $\mathcal{U}=\int(T_h-T)dS$ 在固定 $V$ 时完全相同：因为固定 $V$ 时 $dE=T_hdS$，故 $\int(T_h-T)dS=E-TS+\text{const}$。
    [推·代数；算·V2 给出 $(\partial E/\partial S)_V=T$，正是这一步的前提]

77. 因此本方案是 [25791] 所列三种离壳势中的**第三种（$\mathcal{L}$/$P$–$V$ 型、或 Helmholtz 型）实现**，而非 $\mathcal{U}$ 版本；[25791] 自己指出这在 $P$–$V$ 分析中"更自然"，且结论与选择无关。
    [算·第 76 条；引·25791 §III："for G-T/G-P or P-V analyses, 𝒢 or ℒ would be more natural. The physical conclusions are equivalent regardless of the choice."]

78. 与 AdS 情况的差别在于共轭变量：AdS 中 $P=-\Lambda/8\pi$ 是可自由调节的控制参数、$V=\frac43\pi r_+^3$ 由 $r_+$ 决定；腔中 $V=4\pi r_B^2$ 由腔壁决定、$P$ 由 $(r_h,r_B)$ 导出（第 39 条的闭式）。
    [引·25791 式(9) 的 $V=\frac43\pi r_+^3$；引·03749 式(2) 的 $V=4\pi r_B^2$ 与式(5) 的 $P(S,V)$；算·V6]

79. 因此"把 AdS 换成腔壁"在数学上是一次 Helmholtz↔Gibbs 互换（控制变量 $(T,V)$ 与 $(T,P)$ 对调），不是简单的数值替换；这一点必须在论文中写明。
    [推·由第 78 条；引·03749 §II "the independent variables are the temperature $T$ and the volume $V$"（Helmholtz）与 §II 的焓表示（$S,P$）]

Simplify[PowerExpand[(D[Ef[S, V], S] - Tf[S, V]) /. sub], as]

Simplify[PowerExpand[(D[Tf[S, V], S] - Tf[S, V] (3 xx - 2)/(4 S (1 - xx))) /. sub], as]
