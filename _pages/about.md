Welcome! I am **Hengxin Lu**, working on gravitational physics and black hole thermodynamics.

My research centres on what happens once the Bekenstein–Hawking entropy is modified. When black hole entropy receives corrections — from quantum-gravity arguments, from entanglement entropy, or from generalized entropy proposals — an ambiguity often arises: is it the Hawking temperature or the thermodynamic mass that should be modified? The two choices lead to different field equations and to different cosmological consequences. I study this ambiguity, its horizon-area dependence, and its bearing on entropy bounds such as the Bekenstein bound.

## Research interests

- Generalized entropy and its thermodynamic implications
- Black hole thermodynamics and the Bekenstein bound
- Horizon-area dependent modifications of gravity and their cosmological signatures
